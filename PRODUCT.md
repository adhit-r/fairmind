# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FairMind serves AI assurance leads, model-risk and compliance teams, ML engineers, security testers, and independent reviewers. They work across a shared AI-system record while preparing a release decision, investigating a failed evaluation, mapping evidence to controls, or assembling an audit packet.

Their primary job is not to obtain another readiness score. It is to answer five defensible questions: what was tested, against which subject and threshold, what happened, who reviewed the result, and which governance obligations can reuse that evidence.

## Product Purpose

FairMind is a vendor-neutral AI assurance evidence workbench. It converts outputs from model, data, LLM, agent, security, provenance, and environmental evaluators into portable Evidence Passports with an explicit chain of custody.

An Evidence Passport binds the subject, dataset or prompt scope, evaluator and metric versions, pre-registered threshold, raw artifact integrity, result, limitations, reviewer decision, remediation, and superseding rerun. Versioned mappings let one accepted passport support several governance frameworks without pretending that a technical test automatically proves compliance.

Success means an engineer can bring a real test result into FairMind, a reviewer can accept or reject its control mappings, and an assurance lead can export a reproducible evidence packet whose claims can be traced back to immutable source artifacts.

## Brand Personality

Forensic, decisive, interoperable.

The voice is plain, exact, and professionally skeptical. FairMind should feel like a well-run assurance room: direct enough for engineers, legible enough for governance teams, and careful enough for an auditor. It explains uncertainty instead of hiding it behind polished scores.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI governance suites built around inventories, questionnaires, and percentage-readiness dashboards.
- Generic AI tool marketing, chat-first copilots, floating sparkle icons, and feature claims without test evidence.
- Purple gradients, neon-on-black cyber styling, glassmorphism, soft card clouds, and ornamental animation.
- Compliance certificates or green pass states inferred from missing data, provider availability, or unreviewed mappings.
- Isolated test forms that return transient results without subject identity, provenance, artifact hashes, or a review path.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before score.** Every summary must open into the concrete artifacts and decisions that produced it.
2. **One system context.** Inventory, tests, evidence, findings, remediation, approvals, and reports stay anchored to the selected organization and AI system.
3. **Make chain of custody visible.** Subject version, test version, threshold, artifact hash, reviewer, and rerun history are first-class interface content.
4. **Keep the human review boundary.** Evaluators may propose mappings. Only an accountable reviewer may accept evidence for a control or release decision.
5. **Expose capability truth.** Every evaluator is visibly marked as validated, metadata-only, external-provider, unavailable, or insufficient-data.
6. **Reuse evidence, not claims.** One passport may support several versioned framework mappings, but FairMind never auto-declares conformity or certification.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use persistent text labels with icons, keyboard-complete workflows, visible focus states, 44px minimum touch targets, non-color status cues, and tables that retain meaning at high zoom. Respect reduced motion and never require animation to understand a state change. Use plain-language summaries alongside technical metrics without removing the underlying data.
