product

## Users

FairMind serves four primary user groups:

- AI governance and compliance leads who need a current, defensible view of control readiness across models and agents.
- Model and agent owners who run evaluations, explain failures, attach implementation evidence, and resolve findings.
- Organization administrators who provision the company workspace, integrations, roles, and access.
- Internal reviewers and external auditors who trace a framework requirement through controls, evidence, findings, remediation, and decisions.

Users work in a high-consequence review workflow. They need reliable scope, provenance, ownership, and next actions more than decorative dashboards.

## Product Purpose

FairMind is an AI assurance platform. Its AI Governance module inventories AI systems, applies versioned governance frameworks, evaluates models and agents, collects evidence from company integrations, and prepares review-ready assurance packages.

Success means a company can register an AI system, activate a framework such as AIUC-1, understand the applicable controls, produce and review evidence, remediate failures, and hand an auditor a version-pinned evidence trail. FairMind supports readiness and review; it does not automatically certify compliance.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, direct, evidence-led.

FairMind should feel like a serious technical workbench shared by engineers and assurance professionals. The voice is concise and specific. It distinguishes observed facts, evaluator findings, reviewer decisions, and unresolved uncertainty.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards made from interchangeable cards and decorative metrics.
- Generic AI product styling, especially purple gradients, glow effects, glassmorphism, and unexplained automation claims.
- Marketing hero layouts inside authenticated product workflows.
- Compliance theatre that turns weak evidence into a reassuring score or implies automatic certification.
- A direct visual or structural clone of Vanta, Drata, or the AIUC-1 mapping microsite.
- Soft, playful interfaces that obscure ownership, severity, provenance, or review state.

## Design Principles

1. **System first, framework aware.** The governed model, agent, or composite application is the primary object. Frameworks are reusable lenses over shared controls and evidence.
2. **Trace every claim.** Readiness statements must lead to control definitions, evidence, evaluation runs, findings, remediation, and decisions.
3. **Show work before scores.** Prioritize blockers, owners, freshness, failed tests, and review state over a single compliance percentage.
4. **Reuse evidence deliberately.** One reviewed artifact may support several controls, but each relationship carries its own rationale and reviewer disposition.
5. **Automation proposes, people decide.** Integrations and FairMind evaluations create evidence and candidate mappings. Reviewers accept mappings and control conclusions.
6. **Keep the task surface dense and calm.** Strong hierarchy, predictable tables, compact context, and contextual actions beat more pages and more cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All governance work must be possible with a keyboard, visible focus, semantic headings and tables, non-color status labels, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion support, and responsive views that preserve evidence and control context. Exported reports must retain readable hierarchy without relying on color alone.
