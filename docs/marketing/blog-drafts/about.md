Chain of Custody is a set of guides to AI regulation, security and safety. We write for the people who have to show an auditor their work: compliance leads, security engineers and ML engineers who need to know what a rule requires and by when.

## Who writes the guides

The guides are written by Adhithya Rajasekaran at Axonome, the company that builds FairMind, an agentic AI governance platform. FairMind publishes the blog. The guides explain public texts and are not product documentation, although each one ends with a short note on where FairMind fits.

## How we check sources

Every guide is built from primary sources: the legal text, the official list, or the standard's own public description. Each guide lists those sources at the end and shows the date we last updated it. Where sources disagree on a date or a reading, the guide says so and gives both rather than picking one silently.

ISO and IEC standards are copyrighted, so guides on them describe what public sources say the standard covers and do not reproduce its text.

## Updates and corrections

Rules change and we sometimes get things wrong. When that happens we correct the guide in place and update its date, so the page you cite is the current one. The Deadlines page lists the dates we are tracking and is updated as official notices appear.

## What the guides are not

The guides are engineering readings of public texts. They are not legal advice, and they do not certify compliance with any framework. Check anything you act on with your own counsel.
