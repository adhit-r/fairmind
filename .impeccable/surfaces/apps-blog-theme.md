# Chain of Custody blog (Ghost theme)

Scope: blog.fairmind.xyz home, topic (tag), guide (post), page and deadlines views. Visitor mode: Read.
Audience: compliance leads asking what an AI rule requires and by when. Success: they trust and cite a guide, request FairMind access, subscribe to The Custody Log.
Constraints: no italics, no emoji, no neobrutalism, no newspaper costume, no SaaS hero, real data only (Ghost posts, tags, docket page).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A packed reference portal where every framework, guide and deadline is one glance away; it refuses the content-marketing hero with a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, #111 ink, utility red #E60012 for tabs, labels and urgent numerals, grays F2F2F2/E0E0E0/D6D6D6/7A7A7A. Hairline-ruled modules with small headers and "View all" links, red block rail header, red-underline tab nav, condensed bold display numerals, compact gothic sans UI, outlined secondary buttons, 1.5px line icons.

STORY: The reader sees every framework and the next deadlines at once, opens the guide they need, reads it in a calm column, and meets FairMind and the newsletter in side modules.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with wordmark, wide search and Subscribe; tab row of frameworks; left rail of frameworks with counts; centre search module with headline and popular searches; New guides and Coming due modules to the right.

FORM: High-density index (japanese-high-density-web challenger, dealt by the roll; assigned index 4 declined by the user). seed: 59d00918a88df1c8

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
