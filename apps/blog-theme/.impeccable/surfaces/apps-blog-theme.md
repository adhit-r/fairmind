# Chain of Custody blog (Ghost theme)

Scope: blog.fairmind.xyz home, guide (post), framework (tag), frameworks index, deadlines, about and author views. Visitor mode: Read.
Audience: compliance leads asking what an AI rule requires and by when. Success: they keep reading, trust and cite a guide, subscribe to The Custody Log, request FairMind access.
Constraints: no italics, no emoji, no neobrutalism (no boxes, block fills or hard shadows), no newspaper costume, no SaaS hero, real data only. Owner rejected the high-density portal (seed 59d00918a88df1c8) after an impeccable critique scored it 25/40: portal not blog, brutal surface, crowded guide page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm publication that hooks readers into the next guide; it refuses the widget dashboard and the newspaper costume alike.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white ground, soft ink, one deep green accent for links and actions, a single warm red reserved for deadlines inside 30 days. No boxes: whitespace and hairline rules only. Literata (self-hosted) for headlines and long reading, the platform sans (system-ui) for meta and controls, upright throughout. Light and dark from the reader's system.

STORY: The reader meets one strong lead guide, scrolls one dated feed, opens a guide, reads in a quiet column, and finishes on a single ending that offers the next guide, the citation, the author and the newsletter.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim masthead (name, one-line description, Latest, Frameworks, Deadlines, About, search, Subscribe); the lead guide large in serif with topic, dek, byline and read time; one quiet next-deadline line; the feed begins below.

FORM: Calm editorial publication, pinned by the owner (tone: calm editorial; structure: built to hook readers and lengthen reading). seed:b9bec896dcb313cb (roll run for the record; the owner's pinned direction overrides its assignment)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
