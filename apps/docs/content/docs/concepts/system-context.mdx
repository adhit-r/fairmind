Every FairMind workflow begins with an organization and an AI system.

## Organization

The organization is the membership and administration boundary. Members, invitations, roles, settings, and audit records are managed in organization-scoped routes.

## Workspace and AI system

Onboarding creates a workspace and then a system record. The system carries the governance context used across risks, evidence, remediation, approvals, and reports.

Keep the selected system visible while working. A technically correct artifact attached to the wrong subject is not defensible evidence.

## Models and datasets

Models and datasets are inventory objects associated with a system and its evaluations. Recording inventory metadata does not prove the integrity or behavior of the referenced asset.

## Local persistence

The dashboard stores the selected organization and system in browser storage for continuity. A fallback system may appear when the API returns no systems or fails.

<Callout type="warn" title="Demo and fallback data">
Treat fallback or fixture content as demonstration data. Do not present it as an observed customer system or a measured production result.
</Callout>
