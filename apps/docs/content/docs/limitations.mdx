FairMind is an active product with uneven maturity across evaluators and governance surfaces.

## Current product boundaries

- Environmental governance is the strongest evidence-producing vertical, but benchmarks remain provisional.
- Evidence records are mutable and confidence may be user-recorded.
- Tabular bias is unavailable as an evidence-grade evaluator in the active flow.
- Modern LLM bias uses demonstration results.
- Multimodal bias and AI BOM are metadata-only.
- Explainability Studio and Security Testing are interface-only.
- LLM judge requires an external provider and may transmit input outside your deployment.
- The governance report path stores JSON snapshots; the active export route does not produce signed PDF or DOCX packages.
- Authentication and RBAC are transitional and include development-only behavior.
- Research fixtures and paper results do not establish general product performance.

## Safe use

1. Confirm the capability state before running an evaluator.
2. Inspect the underlying artifact, scope, threshold, provenance, and limitations.
3. Keep a human reviewer in the decision path.
4. Treat framework mappings as evidence relevance, not automatic conformity.
5. Preserve rerun and remediation history.
6. Validate production authentication, tenant isolation, and deployment controls in your own environment.
