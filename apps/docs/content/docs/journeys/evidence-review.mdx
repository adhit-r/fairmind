## Record the artifact

1. Select the correct organization and system.
2. Open **Evidence & Controls > Evidence Workbench**.
3. Add a concise title and evidence type.
4. Identify the source or evaluator and the subject version.
5. Attach a file reference, URL, or structured content.
6. Record confidence only when its meaning is defined.
7. Add limitations, tags, folder, and uploader context.

## Review before mapping

Check the artifact against four questions:

- Does it identify the governed subject?
- Is the evaluator or source traceable?
- Are the method, threshold, and scope clear?
- Are limitations and missing data explicit?

If any answer is no, keep the record in review and do not use it as release evidence.

## Link, do not overclaim

Link the evidence to the relevant risk, control, remediation task, or evaluation. A link records relevance. It does not automatically satisfy the control.

## Evidence basis

Evidence list, collection, update, and link operations are defined in `apps/frontend/src/lib/api/hooks/useEvidence.ts` and persisted by `apps/backend/src/api/routers/ai_governance.py`.
