## What it is for

A policy here is more than a document. It has a status, rules the platform can check, and a record of who acknowledged it. This page shows all of that, and which rules fail on which systems.

## How to use it

1. Open **Policies**. The facts row counts published, in review or approved, drafts, acknowledgements and open alerts.
2. Use the tabs **All** and **With open alerts** to filter. Each shows a count.
3. Read the table. Columns are **Policy**, **Status**, **Rules**, **Acknowledged**, **Enforcement** and **Alerts**. Status is Draft, In review, Approved, Published or Retired. **Enforcement** reads Not enforced, Passing, or N failing. The small line under a name gives its version, the version in force if different, and the owner.
4. Click a policy name to open it.
5. Press **Enforce rules now** to check every published rule against every system. A banner then reports how many passed, failed, were blocking, or did not apply.
6. Press **Draft a policy with the agent**. In the dialog, write **What should the policy say?** (at least 10 characters) and press **Draft policy**. The result names the policy, how many rules and controls it maps, and how many suggestions it dropped because the platform cannot check them. Press **Review it** to open the draft, then submit it for approval.
7. Under **Open alerts**, each failing rule shows **Blocking** or **Warning**, the system, the detail and when it started failing. Press **Open the system**, or **Acknowledge** to record that you have seen it. **All alerts** goes to the full list.

## What's on the page

A blocking rule can stop a release approval. A warning does not. Acknowledging an alert records that someone saw it. It does not make the rule pass.

Policies in review, and policies you have not read, also appear in [Needs you](../home/needs-you.mdx). A policy's own page, reached from its name, shows that policy in full.

With no policies the table says **No policies yet**. If the list cannot load, **Policies could not load** appears with **Try again**.
