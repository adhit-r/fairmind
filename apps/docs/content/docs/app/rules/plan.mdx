## What it is for

The plan turns the frameworks you answer to into tasks. Each task is a control, with an owner, the systems it covers, and what proves it. The page has two states: setting the plan up, and working it.

## How to use it

**Set it up.** Until a plan exists the page shows **Set up your governance plan**.

1. Answer each question. The kind of question decides the control: type a sentence and press **Send**, press one option, tick several and press **Continue**, or set a level for each control.
2. When asked about frameworks, tick the suggested ones, add others with **Add another framework** and **Add**, then press **Confirm frameworks**. FairMind pre-ticks from your inventory where it can.
3. The side panel, **What I have mapped**, fills in as you answer and says whether each fact is from your answer, your description or your inventory.
4. When no question is left, the page says **I have what I need** and counts the controls. Press **Build my plan**. It will not build before frameworks are confirmed.

**Work it.** The heading then reads "N of M controls proven", with a progress bar.

1. Read the five lanes: **To do**, **Needs you**, **Agent working**, **In review** and **Proven**. Press **Refresh** to re-read the plan.
2. Click a card to open the task. It shows the control id, priority and objective.
3. Under **Owner**, type a name or email in **Assign to** and press **Assign**. If a suggested owner exists the button reads **Accept suggestion**.
4. Write context under **Note** and press **Save note**.
5. For an organisation-level task, **Already in place?** lets you record what exists. Write **What is in place, and since when** (at least 12 characters), optionally **Where the evidence is**, then press **Attest**. It counts as attested, not measured.

## What's on the page

A task drawer also lists the systems it covers with their progress, what the task **Comes after**, **How to do it** (workflows, and whether their pack is installed), and **Requirements it closes** grouped by framework.

A **What changed** banner shows the latest change to the plan. If the plan cannot load, the page reads **The plan could not load** with **Try again**.
