# Limitations and roadmap

P0 completes the trustworthy-control-plane foundation, not the complete AI
assurance platform. Its records are useful only when the surrounding deployment
and operational controls are actually in place.

## Current limitations

- Assurance V2 and every child capability gate default to disabled.
- There is no released worker execution, queue, sandbox, adapter runtime,
  artifact broker, or real evaluation engine.
- FairMind-worker delivery preflight is deliberately blocked; automatic
  enforcement is deliberately rejected.
- The dashboard evidence-trust view is an internal, read-only, default-off
  preview. It is not a substitute for server-side authorization or evidence.
- The P0 evidence foundation does not validate fairness, safety, performance,
  robustness, security, emissions, or legal compliance for an AI system.
- Results depend on the integrity, completeness, scope, and freshness of the
  evidence an approved evaluator supplies.
- Legal and regulatory mapping is an organizational activity. P0 can organize
  supporting evidence, but does not certify compliance or conformity.

## Delivery sequence after P0

The roadmap proceeds through distinct, independently verifiable areas:

1. **Isolated workers:** service-principal lifecycle, queue and lease controls,
   execution isolation, cancellation, artifacts, and delivery integrity.
2. **Real evaluation engines:** reproducible predictive-model, LLM, agent,
   code, and other domain-specific evaluation implementations with benchmark
   evidence.
3. **Modality packs:** independently validated image, audio, video, vision, and
   multimodal work rather than a shared label alone.
4. **Lifecycle assurance:** pre-deployment, realtime, and post-deployment
   workflows with explicit operating evidence.
5. **Verification and rollout gates:** integration, security, runtime,
   benchmark, usability, and release evidence before capability claims expand.

Completion of one area does not automatically make another available. Each
requires its own contracts, controls, tests, operational evidence, and release
decision.

## How to communicate the current state

Say “internal, default-off assurance control plane” when referring to P0.
State the exact evidence status and limitations alongside any decision record.
Do not describe P0 as a live evaluator, continuous monitoring product,
automated release gate, compliance tool, or certification service.
