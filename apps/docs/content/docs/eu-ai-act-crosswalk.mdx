# EU AI Act evidence-support crosswalk

This page is an evidence-support guide, not legal advice, a conformity
assessment, a certification, or a determination that the EU AI Act applies to
your system. The organization using or providing an AI system remains
responsible for role, risk-category, and obligation analysis with qualified
counsel or compliance specialists.

Start with the European Commission’s [AI Act framework and timeline](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), the [current consolidated Act text on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng), and the [original Official Journal text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj?locale=en). Check EUR-Lex for a newer consolidation before relying on the text.
The Commission’s [AI Act Compliance Checker](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/eu-ai-act-compliance-checker)
is an informational beta tool, not a Commission assessment of a specific case.

## Start with applicability

Before mapping any FairMind record, document at least:

1. Your role: provider, deployer, importer, distributor, authorized
   representative, or general-purpose AI provider.
2. The system’s intended purpose, users, deployment context, geography, and
   affected people.
3. Whether a prohibition, high-risk classification, transparency obligation,
   general-purpose AI obligation, or another EU-law obligation may apply.
4. The owner of the legal analysis, the decision date, supporting sources, and
   unresolved questions.

P0 does not perform this classification.

## What P0 can support

| AI Act area | P0 evidence support | Important limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Risk-management process, Article 9 | Versioned plans, lifecycle labels, evidence states, and decision records can organize a risk-control workflow. | P0 does not establish a complete risk-management system or prove risk reduction. |
| Technical documentation, Article 11 and Annex IV | Immutable target, suite, configuration, envelope, and evidence hashes can support traceability. | The organization must determine and create the required technical documentation. |
| Record-keeping/logging, Article 12 | Append-only admissions, links, reviews, decisions, idempotency, and audit records can support an evidence trail. | Retention, export, accessibility, and legal record requirements are not proven by P0. |
| Transparency and instructions, Article 13 | Status, limitations, provenance, freshness, and reviewer data can inform internal documentation. | P0 does not supply all user instructions or transparency content. |
| Human oversight, Article 14 | Four-eyes evidence review and a separately gated, audited decision exception support separation and accountability. | P0 does not supply runtime override, stop, or operational oversight procedures. |
| Accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity, Article 15 | Scope, signature, replay, and trust controls support evidence integrity. | P0 does not provide validated model-performance, robustness, adversarial, or cybersecurity evaluation results. |
| Quality-management system, Article 17 | Controlled records may be inputs to a QMS. | P0 is not a complete QMS. |
| Deployer obligations and FRIA, including Articles 26–27 | P0 can organize supporting evidence and decisions. | It does not determine deployer duties or complete a fundamental-rights impact assessment. |
| GPAI provider obligations, including Articles 53 and 55 | Versioned evidence structures can support a provider’s own dossiers. | P0 does not produce the required GPAI technical documentation, training-content summary, systemic-risk evaluation, or incident reporting. |

## Evidence quality questions

For each claim in a compliance dossier, record the exact run, suite, target,
evaluator identity, source artifacts, signer, review status, freshness state,
limitations, and decision owner. If any is unavailable, say so; do not convert
an incomplete record into a compliance conclusion.

## Current legal guidance can change

The Commission’s [AI Act FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/navigating-ai-act)
and its [guidance for GPAI providers](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-gpai-providers)
should be checked again when making an operational or legal decision. Dates,
implementing rules, guidance, and applicability details may change.
