# Research Submission Strategy

## Current Project Signal
Fairness and AI governance platform with docs and paper-oriented material. Most promising if narrowed to code-generation bias or machine-readable compliance evidence.

Classification: **product/system that can become a paper**.

Local evidence sampled:
- `README.md`
- `docs/README.md`
- `docs/paper`

## Candidate Research Framing
**Agent-Native Fairness Auditing and Regulatory Evidence for AI Systems**

Target community: AI governance, fairness auditing, policy evidence.

Recommended framing: position this folder as a supporting or reachable applied-research candidate.

## Strengths
- Touches fairness, AI compliance, evidence collection, and governance.
- Can connect bias metrics to regulatory artifacts, which is a recognized gap in practical AI governance.
- Journal of Cybersecurity fit improves if societal and organizational risk is explicit.

## Gaps
- Broad platform claims need validation and may look unfocused.
- Needs audited implementation status for every claimed metric and framework.
- Must avoid claiming first-of-kind without detailed prior-art comparison.

## What To Add Next
- Narrow first paper to one measurable workflow: generated-code bias, compliance evidence mapping, or AI BOM risk.
- Add labeled benchmark, statistical tests, and baseline tools such as AIF360/Fairlearn.
- Publish evidence schema and reproducible examples.

## Journal Fit
| Journal | Fit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IEEE TDSC | Weak unless system-security evaluation is added | Best only for rigorous system-security design, formal threat model, strong experiments, and dependable/security evaluation. |
| IEEE TIFS | Weak unless reframed as detection/forensics | Best only for detection, forensics, adversarial ML, privacy/security analytics, or multimedia/signal security. |
| JISA | Good applied-security fallback | Best for applied information security, privacy, secure coding, DLP, and practice-driven systems with reproducible evaluation. |
| Cybersecurity | Possible if real-world cyber evaluation is added | Best for real-world cyber defense, AI-for-cyber, SOC, network security, and practical deployment evaluation. |
| Journal of Cybersecurity | Best fit | Best for interdisciplinary cyber risk, governance, human/organizational factors, privacy, and policy implications. |
